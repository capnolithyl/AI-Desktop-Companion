Project: Mikan v02 bust-up puppet

Goal:
Create a clean, front-facing, bust-up 2D puppet version of Mikan for Iki/Codex. This version should prioritize fidelity to Mikan’s established design over experimentation. The result should look like the same character across all parts, not multiple interpretations.

Character identity:
Mikan is a mature orange-tabby catgirl desktop companion. She should read as a warm, playful, slightly teasing young adult anime woman. Her design should feel cute, cozy, and polished, not overly exaggerated or childish.

Primary appearance:
- Long, voluminous, wavy orange hair
- Amber / golden eyes
- Soft freckles across the cheeks and nose
- Two black X-shaped hairpins on the viewer-left side of her bangs
- One small ahoge curl on top of her head
- Two large orange cat ears with soft cream inner fur
- One fluffy orange-striped tabby tail
- Soft, friendly, gentle smile

Outfit:
- Oversized cream / beige off-shoulder cable-knit sweater
- Black lace bra visible subtly at the neckline
- Black choker with gold paw-shaped bell charm
- For v02 bust-up scope, only the upper body is needed; shorts may be excluded unless needed for a preview

Style target:
- Polished anime illustration
- Clean linework
- Soft shading
- Stable facial design
- Suitable for a 2D VTuber / desktop companion puppet
- Should stay visually close to the existing Mikan portrait

Reference priorities:
1. ref-01-portrait.png = primary identity reference
   Use for face, expression vibe, eye shape/color, hair style, personality, and overall “this is Mikan”.
2. ref-02-tpose.png = body / outfit construction reference
   Use for sweater, choker, proportions, and clean clothing structure.
3. ref-03-frontview.png = neutral assembly target
   Use for straight-on front-facing placement, neutral composition, and final assembled layout.

Important constraints:
- Exactly 2 cat ears only
- Exactly 1 tail only
- No duplicate ears hidden inside hair
- No extra hair masses that change the hairstyle dramatically
- Do not make the hair shorter, puffier, or more salon-styled than the references
- The face should not be wider, flatter, or more expressionless than the portrait
- The irises must fit naturally inside the eyes; do not make the irises larger than the visible eye shape
- Keep the expression gentle and alive, not blank, dead-eyed, or unsettling
- Keep Mikan looking like the same character in all parts

Pose / composition:
- Front-facing
- Neutral bust-up / upper torso puppet
- Relaxed posture
- Centered
- Straight-on angle
- No extreme tilt
- No dramatic perspective
- No hands in frame
- No complex background

Required neutral parts only for v02 initial pass:
- face_base
- ear_left
- ear_right
- hair_back
- hair_front
- body_upper
- eye_left_open
- eye_right_open
- iris_left
- iris_right
- mouth_neutral

What success looks like:
The assembled neutral preview should look like the same Mikan from the portrait, but in a clean, front-facing puppet-ready bust-up view. If any single part drifts from that identity, regenerate only that part rather than redesigning the entire character.
